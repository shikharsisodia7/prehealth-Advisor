Department of Physical Therapy

Important information regarding the CSULB Department of Physical Therapy response to COVID-19

The mission of the Doctor of Physical Therapy Program at CSULB, is to advance physical therapy practice through high quality teaching and research engagement to prepare patient-centered, highly competent clinicians who integrate the best scientific evidence into patient care to enhance the health, function, and quality of life of a diverse community.

Learn more about our graduate outcomes here  or click the Commission on Accreditation in Physical Therapy Education (CAPTE) to view our accreditation status.
