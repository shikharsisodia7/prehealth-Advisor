Brenau Online

You will receive an impactful, flexible education
Home Admissions Brenau Online
Apply Today

You don’t need to put your life on hold to earn a degree. With Brenau’s flexible online programs, you can pursue your education without having to rearrange your life. Since launching our first online programs in 1998, Brenau has continued to improve and create new programs, to meet the demands of students’ diverse needs and lifestyles. We offer a convenient, easy-to-use online learning system, where you can attend classes and complete assignments at the time and place that works best for you.

Apply today

Select one of the online programs below for specific admissions instructions and requirements.

Not ready to apply? Request more information and an enrollment counselor will contact you.
