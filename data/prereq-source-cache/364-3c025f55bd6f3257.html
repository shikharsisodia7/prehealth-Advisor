Apply to Augusta University

At Augusta University, you notice your life changing from day one. You discover classes that challenge you, meet faculty members who inspire you, and dive into work that fulfills you. You make friends from around the city, the country, and the world. And thanks to all those hands-on, real-world opportunities that we’re known for, you grow more skillful, knowledgeable, curious, and confident every day.

We invite you to join JagNation and experience a real education for your real life.

Undergraduate Graduate Non-Degree

AU is still accepting applications for Summer 2026 for enrollment beginning on June 26. See more information on parts of term here. The application will close for all summer terms on June 12.

 

Any individual who applies for admission to Augusta University shall pay a non-refundable application fee. Please note that for Health Science, Graduate, and Professional programs, you must be on track or have completed requirements in accordance with the specific program. If you do not meet pre-requisites and are applying for regular admission, please select the appropriate degree seeking category.

Undergraduate

The application fee is $60.

Incoming Freshman

An incoming freshman is any senior in high school or a student who has finished high school and has not attended another college since high school.

Incoming Freshman

Transfer, Readmission, or Additional Undergraduate Degree

This application is for transfer students, life enrichment students, returning former AU students, or those pursuing an additional undergraduate degree. 

Transfer, Readmission, or Additional Undergraduate Degree

Dual Enrollment

This option is for high school students interested in Dual Enrollment at Augusta University. The application fee is waived for these applicants; simply select "Pay Later or Fee Waiver" on the payment screen. A fee waiver code isn't required. 

Dual Enrollment

Capacity Limited Allied Health Science Programs

Applicants for allied health programs (Clinical Laboratory Science, Dental Hygiene, Health Information Administration, Nuclear Medicine Technology, Radiation Therapy, and Respiratory Therapy) — including transfer students and current AU students

Capacity Limited Allied Health Science Programs

Nursing Program

Applicants for the nursing program (via NursingCAS) — including transfer students and current AU students

Nursing Program

Graduate

Applications for Graduate Programs

Graduate Programs

This application is designed for all students applying to graduate programs at AU except Allied Health & Nursing programs.

Graduate Programs

Speech-Language Pathology

All students applying to the Speech Language Pathology program should apply through CSDCAS.

Speech-Language Pathology

Physician Assistant

All students applying to the Physician Assistant program should apply through CASPA.

Physician Assistant

Nursing

All students applying to graduate level Nursing programs should apply through NursingCAS.

Nursing

Physical Therapy

All students applying to the Physical Therapy program should apply through PTCAS.

Physical Therapy

Allied Health, Nutrition

All students applying to the Allied Health, Nutrition program should apply through DICAS.

Allied Health, Nutrition

Non-Degree or Certificate

The application fee is $60.

Transient

A transient applicant is an undergraduate student in good standing at another college or university who wishes to take classes temporarily at Augusta University.

Transient

Non-Degree Undergraduate

Undergraduate non-degree seeking applicants are students seeking to earn credit for undergraduate courses, but are not seeking to complete a degree. A previously-earned bachelor’s degree is required.

Non-Degree Undergraduate

Non-Degree Graduate

Graduate non-degree seeking applicants are students seeking to further their education in graduate courses, but are not seeking to complete a degree. 

Non-Degree Graduate

Certificates

All students seeking admission to a certificate program should apply here.

Certificates

Auditor

All students seeking to audit undergraduate courses, but are not seeking to complete a degree. An Auditor Student attends classes as a non-credit, non-degree seeking student, and must be at least a high school graduate or the equivalent. Auditor students pay full tuition and fees for courses.

Auditor

Statement of Non-Discrimination

Augusta University is an affirmative action/equal opportunity educational institution in that no person shall on the grounds of sex/gender, race/ethnicity, color, creed, religion, age, national origin, sexual orientation, veteran’s status, handicap, or any other category protected by law be excluded from participation in or be otherwise subjected to discrimination in any educational program, activity, or facility.

Augusta University is committed to an effective affirmative action policy. Augusta University strives to recruit, admit and educate a cross-section of qualified men and women representing the socio-economic, racial and cultural diversity of Georgia. This commitment is in keeping with our moral, legal and social responsibility and with the highest ideals and principles of American higher education.

Augusta University
