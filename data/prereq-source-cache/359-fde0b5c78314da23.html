School of Physical Therapy & Rehabilitation Sciences

The mission of the School of Physical Therapy & Rehabilitation Sciences is to "Move Lives Forward" through the education of students in an educational, research, and clinical environment that embraces everyone, innovation, discovery, knowledge, expertise, leadership, collaboration, and partnerships.

Our School houses the Doctor of Physical Therapy Degree program, Professional Athletic Training Program, & Orthopaedic Residency Program. Not only do we provide excellent educational opportunities, but we have a growing research enterprise advancing our knowledge, a physical therapy center providing clinical care to Tampa Bay, and various service opportunities giving back to our communities.

Our unique placement within the USF Health Morsani College of Medicine allows us unmatched collaboration and synergy in education, clinical care, and research. Together we are all Making Life Better and Moving Lives Forward.
